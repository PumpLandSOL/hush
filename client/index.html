Ask the Counter
questions, answered straight
What is hUSD?
A dollar that spends like cash. hUSD is a fractional-algorithmic stablecoin on Robinhood Chain: each unit is minted from USDG, most of which is held as collateral while the remaining share buys and shreds $HUSH. The protocol moves that ratio to hold the price at $1.
How does the peg hold?
Below $1 the collateral ratio rises, so more USDG backs each new hUSD and cashing out pays more hard collateral. Above $1 the ratio falls and more of each mint is the algorithmic share. Cash out is always open, so arbitrage pulls the price back to $1. The mechanism is public and the ratio is on the House Totals slip at all times.
What does “shield” actually do?
It moves hUSD from your on-the-books balance into the shielded pool and writes a note encrypted only to you (x25519) plus a commitment in the Merkle tree. Nothing else is recorded. Your balance reads ████ to everyone but you.
What can an observer see when I pay quietly?
One nullifier spent and one new commitment added. No amount, no sender, no recipient. The recipient decrypts their note with their own key; nobody else can.
Can I go back on the books?
Yes. Unshield returns a private note to your public balance at any time, and Cash out burns public hUSD for your USDG collateral plus the $HUSH share. Quiet when you choose, public when you choose.
What is $HUSH for?
$HUSH is the share token. A slice of every mint buys $HUSH and shreds it, every service charge buys more and shreds that too, and coupons and Happy Hour pay out in it. Demand for private dollars flows into $HUSH. It is also the governance token for the shifts ahead.
Is my USDG actually deposited?
Yes. A deposit is a real USDG transfer on Robinhood Chain to the treasury. The server reads the transaction receipt, checks that the Transfer log runs from your wallet to the treasury, and only then credits your tab. Every deposited dollar sits in a treasury address you can open on the explorer.
How do I get USDG back out?
Cash out hUSD for USDG on your tab, then Withdraw. Withdrawals enter a queue the treasury pays from a cold wallet, usually within 24 hours, and the payout transaction hash is written next to your request. There is deliberately no hot key on the server.
What is an Envelope?
A pay link. You tuck shielded hUSD behind a random secret and share the link. Whoever opens it connects a wallet and the hUSD lands in their shielded balance. The secret lives only in the link fragment; the server stores a hash of it, and the ledger shows one nullifier and one commitment, indistinguishable from any quiet payment. One claim per envelope.
What is the Carbon Copy?
A view key: an HMAC over your address with a server salt, encoded together with your address so the statement page can verify it. It reads your shielded balance and history and has no spending power. This is the selective-disclosure model privacy coins use for audits: you choose who sees, and only they see.
How do Regulars work?
When someone opens your Envelope or connects through your link (hushrh.xyz/?ref=your-address) for the first time, they become your regular. 20% of every service charge they pay from then on is credited to your public hUSD, forever. The other 80% still goes to the Shredder. One referrer per wallet, set once, never changed.
What is the Back Room?
Private exposure to tokenized stocks. You put shielded hUSD on a long or short in HOOD, TSLA, NVDA, SPY and others at 1x, priced off the live exchange tape. When you close, P&L settles back into your shielded balance. Nobody can see what you hold, how much, or which way. Positions are capped per wallet and house-wide, shorts stop out at a 95% loss, and orders only fill while the tape is fresh. The house is the counterparty; fees go to the Shredder.
What are Coupons?
The fastest way to grow the register. Deposit USDG and receive $HUSH at 20% below market, vesting linearly over 5 days, claimable as it vests and withdrawable to your wallet from the treasury. Your USDG goes into the hUSD reserve and mints nothing, so coupons push the collateral ratio up. Capacity is capped per day and the program closes with Happy Hour.
What is the Freezer?
A locked coupon. Take $HUSH at 30% below market, locked for 48 hours instead of vesting over 5 days. While frozen it earns 80% APY, paid in $HUSH from the same fixed pool that funds Happy Hour. At thaw you claim principal plus yield in one click. The USDG goes to the reserve and mints nothing.
What is Happy Hour, and why does it end?
A limited staking window: stake hUSD and earn a fixed APY paid in $HUSH from a pool set aside in advance. It has a hard end date and a hard pool size. Permanent subsidised yield is what turned Anchor into the only reason to hold UST. A yield that ends cannot become load-bearing.
What is the Shredder?
The buyback. Every shield, quiet payment and unshield pays 30 bps, every cash out pays 50 bps. Charges collect in USDG and each run the protocol buys $HUSH at market and shreds it, printing a public receipt. Use of private money becomes permanent scarcity in the share token.
Which chain and which wallet?
Robinhood Chain (chainId 4663). Connect any EVM wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby; the site switches the network for you. USDG is the collateral asset.
How is risk handled?
The collateral ratio is published live, cash out is always open, yield programs have fixed pools and end dates, and Back Room exposure is capped per wallet and house-wide. Withdrawals are paid from a cold wallet, so no key on the server can move funds. A peg is a mechanism, and shielded notes are encrypted to your keys alone, so keep your keys and size your tab sensibly.
